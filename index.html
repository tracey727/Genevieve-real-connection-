FRIENDSHIP → SOMETHING MORE?

The Mutual Step Principle

Invite. Notice. Ask. Match pace. No mind-reading, no pressure, no forced timetable.

Green — continue naturally

Mutual initiation, consistency, warmth, respected boundaries, room for independence, easy repair after small misunderstandings.

Yellow — slow down

You are doing most of the work, intensity is racing ahead of knowledge, communication is inconsistent, or you are guessing instead of asking.

Red — do not progress

Pressure after no, possessiveness, humiliation, monitoring, isolation, threats, sexual pressure, controlling behaviour or fear.

6-stage micro-progression

  1. Familiarity: talk naturally when you see each other.
  2. Deliberate contact: suggest a small, low-pressure activity.
  3. One-to-one time: learn whether you actually enjoy each other's company.
  4. Slightly deeper conversation: values, hopes, humour, experiences — not a life-history dump.
  5. Reciprocity check: are they moving toward you too?
  6. Name the possibility: only when there is a pattern, ask rather than assume.

PRIVACY & SAFETY

Your trial stays on this device

  • This prototype uses browser storage only. It does not create public profiles or upload your people/reflection data to a server.
  • Do not store full addresses, passwords, financial account details, diagnoses or another person's sensitive information.
  • Do not use another person's warmth, friendliness or physical proximity as consent to romance or touch. Ask.
  • First one-to-one meetings should be in ordinary public places. Do not reveal that you live alone or are isolated to new people.
  • If someone ignores a clear boundary, the app's job is not to help you "win them over" — it tells you to slow down or stop.
